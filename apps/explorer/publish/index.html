Publish your builds

Record every build of your dApp on Arkiv, so your users can check they are running it. 4 steps, about 5 minutes. No gas and no tokens.

  1. 1Connect
  2. 2Get a key
  3. 3Add to repo
  4. 4First release

01 Connect your wallet

Your wallet proves who owns the app name. It signs a message only: no transaction, no gas.

No wallet yet? Install MetaMask or Rainbow in this browser, then reload.

02 Name your app and get a key

The name is how people find your builds. The first wallet to claim it keeps it.

2 to 40 lowercase letters, digits and dashes.

Your API key

••••••••••••••••••••

Shown once. Save it now as the repo secret HYDRA_API_KEY; you cannot see it again.

03 Add Hydra to your repo

One workflow file. Every push to main then records the build on Arkiv and stores it on Swarm.

Paste this into Claude Code, Cursor or Codex, opened in your repo. It sets the secret, writes the workflow and pushes. It contains your key, so paste it only into your own agent.

Then bind your site, so it can show a match: serve /.well-known/hydra.txt on your domain with the single line hydra-app=<your app name>, and set HYDRA_DOMAINS to your domain in the workflow.
